Painless Rack Configuration

Our modular rack-mount chassis integrate with NineVue for network and RF path loss configuration. Get a single, version controlled source of truth for your test racks. Stop installing, Start testing

Our unique "no-installation / no-driver" software lets you begin using Nine Fives test equipment within 10s of plugging it in. Every product includes a touchscreen for quick lab use.

Choose the interface that fits your test setup.

All of our test equipment can be powered via USB-C or Power over Ethernet (IEEE 802.3af).

Communicate your equipment via either physical port, using a Web UI, REST API, or SCPI.

Enterprise Grade. Developer Ready.


Whether you need the robust structure of NI TestStand and Keysight Connection Expert or the agility of a headless Linux environment, we bridge the gap. Maintain your legacy SCPI workflows on Windows, or switch gears and command the hardware directly via our RESTful API—no drivers required, just curl and get testing.
