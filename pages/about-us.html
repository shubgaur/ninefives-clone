About us

Who we are:

Nine Fives is a YC-backed startup founded by experts with over a decade of experience in RF hardware design and system testing.

Our Mission:

Our mission is to deliver, simple, easy-to-use, performant RF test equipment that engineers want to use.

Every product we ship comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee and 2-year warranty.

Meet the team

  • Noah Levy

    CEO

    Noah is a 6-time kickass award winner at SpaceX and was the responsible engineer for the Falcon 9 Radar Altimeter, Dragon 2 S-Band Transceiver. He designed all of the GPS and S-Band radios on Starship as well as the calibration system for the largest space based phased array (by element count) at SpaceX. He became passionate about improving RF test equipment by serving as the squishy object between a fast-paced company and slow-paced test equipment vendors.

  • Andrew Kurtz

    COO

    Andrew has spent nearly a decade in the aerospace industry with stints at SpaceX, where he was responsible for Payload fairing Integration and later Payload accomodations, and Boeing, where he worked on everything from spacecraft valves to DARPA X-Planes. He brings hands on experience delivering aerospace grade products from first sketch through manufacturing and rigorous quality control.
